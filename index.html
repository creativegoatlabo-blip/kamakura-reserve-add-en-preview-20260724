Notes from Kamakura Chokin Kobo

• If you use a mobile carrier email address, please adjust your settings so that you can receive emails from info@kamakura-chokin.com.

• Please cancel your reservation via our website or by phone at least one week before your reservation date. If you cancel without contacting us, a cancellation fee may apply.

• If you will be late, please call us in advance. Depending on your arrival time and the reservation schedule, we may ask you to change your reservation date.

• Pt900 platinum and K18 gold rings in widths of 4.0 mm or more are special-order items. Due to material availability, please consult with us at least four weeks in advance.

• Payment is made on the day by cash, credit card (VISA, MasterCard, JCB, AMEX, Diners Club, etc.), electronic money, or QR code payment.

• After your reservation is completed, an automatic confirmation email will be sent to the email address you entered. If you do not receive the email within 24 hours, your reservation may not have been processed normally. Please contact us by phone.

• For ring pickup or aftercare visits, advance booking helps us guide you smoothly.

• Please read our privacy policy before booking.

Please read before submitting your reservation.
